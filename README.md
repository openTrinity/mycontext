MyContext is a local-first desktop app for everyday communication and knowledge work. It helps organize information from instant messaging into searchable, reviewable personal context, with AI assistance available when you need it.

## Features

- **Local first**: Your data stays on your device.
- **One workspace**: Connect supported communication channels and explore conversations in one place.
- **Search and connections**: Rediscover important information through full-text search and knowledge relationships.
- **Personal assistance**: Receive suggestions based on the content and settings you choose, while keeping important actions under your control.
- **Flexible configuration**: Configure compatible model gateways, models, and runtime options.
- **Extensible architecture**: Data, retrieval, channel, and AI capabilities are organized as modular components.

The project is under active development, and features and interfaces may continue to evolve. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
